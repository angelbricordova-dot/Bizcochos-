# Design System: Mis Bizcochos

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Piñata Table"**

The app is the party table at a Venezuelan birthday: a white tablecloth with a faint pink tint, fuchsia where something acts, sun yellow where the eye should land, turquoise for bolívares and gold for the cake board and every price. It rejects the grey finance-app ledger with one accent. Numbers are friendly and big where they answer a question (this month's profit, today's rate, a suggested price) and quiet everywhere else.

The refinement round made the table cleaner. On phones the header is the tablecloth itself with the business name in fuchsia; fuchsia gathers on what acts: the filled pill behind the active tab's icon, the primary buttons, the checkbox and switch when on, and the desktop rail. Cards and lists sit on the ground as white plates edged by a 1px pink line; shadows are reserved for things that float over the page. At night the same table is laid on a plum-black cloth, with every role lifted to read on it, chosen per device as Automático, Claro or Oscuro.

Density is a phone in a kitchen: one column, 16px side gutters, 56–64px touch rows, a fixed five-tab bar, and the day's main actions fixed above it. On a computer the same content sits in a centred column beside a fuchsia rail that lists every section. The signature is the torta: a top-down cake donut on a gold board whose slices show where each dollar of a price goes; on Inicio a smaller month torta sets what went to expenses against what she kept.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Tablecloth ground, white plates edged by a 1px line, near-black plum ink; plum-black ground at night.
- Fuchsia on what acts: active tab pill, primary actions, toggles, the desktop rail.
- Two-currency ink: dollars in ink, bolívares in turquoise ink, prices in gold ink.
- Plain label/amount rows instead of stat tiles.
- Bricolage Grotesque for headings and answer figures; system sans for everything read.
- Shadows only on floating things: fixed actions, toasts, tooltips.

## Colors

A warm, saturated party palette on a pink-white ground (plum-black at night); every hue has a job tied to money or place, and none of them is a finance green.

### Primary
- **Piñata Fuchsia** (`fucsia`): the fill colour of action. Primary buttons, the floating action pill, the filled pill behind the active tab icon, checked checkboxes, the switch when on, the range thumb, the goal bar, the desktop rail, the login ground, `accent-color`. White (`blanco`) sits on it in both themes (6.7:1 light, 5.6:1 dark). Hover and press darken to **Deep Fuchsia** (`fucsia-hondo`).
- **Fuchsia Ink** (`fucsia-texto`): fuchsia as text and line. The phone wordmark, links and link buttons, the active tab label, the selected segment's text, focused input borders, focus outlines and the caret. Equal to `fucsia` in light; at night it lifts to a rose pink (`fucsia-texto-oscuro`, 6.6:1 on the ground) while the fill deepens only slightly.
- **Fuchsia Blush** (`fucsia-suave`): the income badge behind a plus sign, the icon disc on Más rows, the "Listo" chip and status, the range track.

### Secondary
- **Sun Yellow** (`amarillo`): today's rate and the current place. The BCV chip in the header, the rate panel on Tasa, the active item in the desktop rail, the undo action on the toast, text selection, the focus ring inside the rail, and the "today" dot on the active Encargos tab. It does not change at night.
- **Bolívar Turquoise Ink** (`turquesa-tinta`): every Bs amount, everywhere, as text (5.9:1 light; `turquesa-tinta-oscuro` 10.3:1 at night). The Cobrar amount is the largest use, at 2rem.
- **Turquoise** (`turquesa`): the bright mid-tone, used only as the empaque slice (`serie-2`); never as text.

### Tertiary
- **Cake-Board Gold** (`oro`): the torta's board ring, the ganancia slice, the goal bar once the goal is reached. Never text. **Board Edge** (`oro-borde`) is the darker stroke around the board.
- **Gold Frosting** (`oro-claro`): fill for the price pill, the suggested-price panel and the "Entregado" status. The board's dashed scallop line keeps the light frosting value in both themes, so the board reads the same at night.
- **Price Gold Ink** (`oro-tinta`): price text. The torta centre value, the compact torta's price, the sticky price strip, the price pill, the suggested price (4.5:1 on Gold Frosting light, 7.8:1 at night).

### Neutral
- **Plum Ink** (`tinta`): body text, dollar amounts, the large figures. Flips to a pink-white (`tinta-oscuro`) at night.
- **Fixed Plum Ink** (`tinta-fija`): ink that stays dark in both themes. Text on Sun Yellow and on white chips (rate chip, rate panel, active rail item, selection) and the toast's ground. **Fixed Muted Ink** (`tinta-fija-tenue`) is the secondary line inside the rate panel; one use.
- **Muted Plum** (`tinta-2`): row labels in summaries, secondary copy, icon buttons, unselected segments, the expense badge.
- **Faded Plum** (`tinta-3`): helper text, list sub-lines, inactive tabs, input affixes, axis labels (5.5:1 light, 6.3:1 at night).
- **Tablecloth** (`fondo`): the page ground and the phone header; the ground strip behind the fixed actions on Inicio. Plum-black `fondo-oscuro` (#170d14) at night; the `theme-color` meta follows the active ground.
- **White Plate** (`fondo-elevado`): lists, blocks, inputs, the tab bar, cards, the torta's inner disc. Raised plum (`fondo-elevado-oscuro`) at night.
- **Pressed Pink** (`fondo-hundido`): sunken wells. Result boxes, segmented track, meter and bar tracks, hover on secondary and icon buttons, active legend row, the save-status pill, neutral chips.
- **Pink Hairline** (`linea`) and **Pink Rule** (`linea-fuerte`): the 1px line is the elevation of this system. Hairline edges every card, list and block and divides rows; the Rule borders inputs, secondary buttons, checkboxes and the switch track at rest.
- **Placeholder** (`placeholder`): input placeholder text only (4.9:1 on White Plate; 5.3:1 at night), so a placeholder never carries information the label does not.
- **White** (`blanco`): fixed white for text and icons on fuchsia, the switch knob, the range thumb ring, the toast text, and the QR ground. **Rail Pink** (`rosa-riel`) is the rail's inactive item text (5.6:1 on fuchsia). **Mist Pink** (`rosa-niebla`) is the toast's close icon; one use.

### Status
- **Loss Red** (`peligro`) on **Loss Blush** (`peligro-suave`): negative profit, a losing slice value, field errors, destructive buttons, the "Atrasado" chip, the "Cancelado" status, a losing price card's border.
- **Notice Amber** (`aviso`) on **Notice Cream** (`aviso-suave`): notes and warnings, the delivery question box, and money owed ("Te deben", "Debe $…"). Inside the rate panel the warning uses **Fixed Notice** (`aviso-fijo`) on a 55% white wash; one use.
- **WhatsApp Green** (`whatsapp`): only the icon on the WhatsApp button, so the button is recognised at a glance; one use, never text, never an amount.

### Chart series
The torta's categorical order is fixed: `serie-1` ingredientes (pink), `serie-2` empaque (turquoise), `serie-3` horno (orange), `serie-4` tu trabajo (violet), `serie-5` merma (blue), `serie-6` ganancia (gold). At night horno and ganancia step one shade deeper (`serie-3-oscuro`, `serie-6-oscuro`); the others hold.

Expenses in charts are **Expense Blue** (`serie-gasto`), a hue no torta slice uses. Sales in the Reportes bar chart are `serie-1`; the month torta on Inicio pairs `serie-gasto` (gastos) with `serie-6` (te quedó).

All sets pass the dataviz palette validator: the torta in light (surface #ffffff) and dark (surface #22151f); ventas/gastos (`serie-1` + `serie-gasto`) and gastos/te quedó (`serie-gasto` + `serie-6`) in both themes. Worst adjacent CVD separation is turquoise against pink (ΔE 8.0, deutan), inside the band that needs a second encoding; slices carry 2.2-unit gaps and a value table. In light, orange (2.5:1) and gold (2.6:1) sit below 3:1 against the surface, so a slice is never the only carrier of its value; on Inicio the swatches beside "Te quedó" and "Gastaste" with their figures are that relief.

### Named Rules
**The Two-Currency Ink Rule.** Bolívares always read in turquoise ink (`turquesa-tinta`), dollars in plum ink, prices in gold ink (`oro-tinta`). The Bs colour wins over any other text colour it sits inside (the stylesheet ends with a doubled `.bs` selector to guarantee it).

**The Text-or-Fill Rule.** Fuchsia as text, outline or focused border is `fucsia-texto`; fuchsia as a filled surface is `fucsia`, with white on it. They match in light and split at night; using the fill token for text fails on the plum-black ground.

**The Fixed Ink Rule.** Text on Sun Yellow, on fixed-white chips and the toast ground uses `tinta-fija`, never `tinta`, because `tinta` turns pale at night and yellow does not.

**The No Finance Green Rule.** Income and expense are told apart by `+` / `−` signs and by the row badge (plus on Fuchsia Blush, minus on Pressed Pink), never by green and red amounts. Red is reserved for a real loss or an error.

**The Sun Is Rare Rule.** Sun Yellow marks only today's rate, where you are, and the one thing to do now (undo). It is never a decorative fill.

**The Fixed Slice Order Rule.** `serie-1` to `serie-6` map to the six cost parts in that order, always; expenses are always `serie-gasto`. Never cycle, reorder, or add a colour without re-running the validator in both themes.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bricolage Grotesque Variable (self-hosted via @fontsource-variable; falls back to ui-rounded, system-ui)
**Body Font:** the system sans stack (system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica Neue)

**Character:** a rounded, slightly quirky grotesque for headings, the wordmark and the figures that answer a question, against the phone's own sans for everything you read and type. Headings get -0.02em tracking and balanced wrapping; every amount uses tabular numerals.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, clamp(2.8rem, 13vw, 4.2rem), 1, -0.03em): the month's "te quedó" on Inicio. **Display tasa** (clamp(2.2rem, 11vw, 3.4rem)) is the same voice for the rate on the yellow panel.
- **Figure** (700, 2rem, 1.1): the converter result and the Cobrar amount in Bs.
- **Figure price** (700, 1.9rem, 1.05): the suggested price and the compact torta's price, both in gold ink.
- **Headline** (650, 1.75rem, 1.15): screen titles.
- **Torta centre** (700, clamp(1.25rem, 6vw, 1.6rem)): the price, or the hovered part, inside the cake.
- **Total** (650, 1.35rem): the total row of a summary, the result-well figure, and the rail wordmark (700).
- **Wordmark** (700, 1.2rem): the business name in the phone header, in fuchsia ink.
- **Row figure** (650, 1.15rem): amounts in the plain summary rows; section and converter headings use the same step.
- **Title large** (650, 1.1rem): the month selector and the sticky price strip's figure.
- **Title** (650, 1.05rem): block legends and headings, price-card titles, the greeting. The large button label (1.05rem, 600 body face) and the payment value on Cobrar share the step.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, 1.5): all reading text and inputs; list titles at 600; button labels at 600. Empty-state copy capped at 44ch.
- **Label** (600, 0.95rem): field labels, segmented options, legend table cells, the rail items, the toast action (700), the WhatsApp preview text (400).
- **Control small** (600, 0.92rem): link buttons and soft links, small buttons, input affixes, notes (500).
- **Caption** (500, 0.9rem): order status pills (600), chart legends, data tables, payment labels on Cobrar.
- **Pequeño** (400, 0.875rem): the `.pequeno` utility for secondary lines inside rows and helper paragraphs.
- **Small** (400, 0.85rem): field helpers and errors (600), list sub-lines, the suggested-price label, chart tooltips.
- **Micro** (600, 0.75rem): the save-status pill and the torta centre label.
- **Tab** (600, 0.72rem): bottom tab labels under 22px icons; status chips at 700; the torta centre sub-line at 400.

One-off sizes in the build, not steps to reuse: the login title (1.8rem), the empty-state heading (1.3rem), group titles (1.02rem, a hair under Title), the data-table header (0.8rem), the rail's order counter (0.78rem) and the "BCV" tag in the rate chip (0.7rem, 700, +0.02em). New work should take the nearest step above.

### Named Rules
**The Answer Speaks Bricolage Rule.** A figure that answers the baker's question (profit, rate, price, row totals, the amount to charge) is set in the display face; amounts inside lists and tables stay in the body face at 600–650. Every amount carries tabular numerals.

**The Spoken Number Rule.** Money reads the Venezuelan way: `$1.234,56` and `Bs. 1.234,56` with two decimals, tiny unit costs kept to three significant digits (`$0,0024`), the rate to four decimals, percentages to one. Signs are `+` and the true minus `−`. Counts choose the singular or plural word ("1 venta", "3 ventas", "1 porción"), never "venta(s)".

## Layout

Mobile-first, single column with 16px side gutters and a 16px rhythm between screen sections (18px inside forms, 10px inside groups, 12px inside sections). The five-tab bar (Inicio, Encargos, Productos, Cuentas, Más) is fixed at the bottom, 64px plus the safe-area inset, edged by a 1px line on top, and content is padded to clear it. Fixed actions sit above the tab bar. On Cuentas and Encargos a floating fuchsia pill sits bottom-right. The product and order editors carry a sticky footer on White Plate with a 1px line; on phones it shows the price in gold ink above the save button.

### Named Rules
**The Rows Not Tiles Rule.** Summaries are label/amount rows, never KPI tiles or cards-per-number.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by line. White plates sit on the Tablecloth and are told apart by a 1px Pink Hairline, never by a shadow; sunken Pressed Pink wells carry the second level. Shadows exist only for what floats over the page, and they are soft and plum-tinted in light, black and deeper at night.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Floating** (`--sombra-alta`: `0 2px 6px rgb(64 12 40 / 0.1), 0 14px 34px rgb(64 12 40 / 0.16)`; at night `0 2px 6px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.4), 0 14px 34px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.45)`): the floating action pill, the undo toast, the chart tooltip.
- **Resting** (`--sombra`: `0 1px 2px rgb(64 12 40 / 0.05), 0 6px 18px rgb(64 12 40 / 0.06)`; at night `0 1px 2px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.3), 0 6px 18px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.25)`): defined for small floating surfaces; nothing at rest wears it.
- **Focus ring** (`--anillo`: `0 0 0 3px rgb(176 24 91 / 0.18)`; at night `0 0 0 3px rgb(240 106 159 / 0.28)`): focused inputs, number fields, search and checkboxes. Other focusable things get a 2.5px Fuchsia Ink outline offset 2px (Sun Yellow inside the rail).
- **Knobs** (`0 1px 3px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.25)` on the switch knob; `0 1px 4px rgb(64 12 40 / 0.35)` on the range thumb) and the **selected segment** (`0 1px 2px rgb(64 12 40 / 0.1), 0 2px 6px rgb(64 12 40 / 0.06)`): small physical lifts inside controls.
- **Login box** (`0 10px 40px rgb(60 0 30 / 0.35)`): the white box floating on the fuchsia login ground; one use.

### Named Rules
**The Line Not Shadow Rule.** Cards, lists, blocks, panels, the tab bar, the sticky footers and the Inicio action strip are separated by a 1px line. A shadow on a resting surface is drift.

**The Floating Earns Shadow Rule.** Only an element positioned over content (fixed or absolutely placed: action pill, toast, tooltip) takes `--sombra-alta`.

## Shapes

Friendly rounded corners from a 12px/16px pair: 16px (`--radio`) for cards, lists, blocks, panels, result wells and the chat bubble; 12px (`--radio-chico`) for buttons, inputs, search, segmented tracks, notes, the suggested-price panel and rail items. Around them a few nested steps: 14px for large buttons, the toast and the QR; 10px for small buttons, the chart tooltip and the QR thumbnail; 9px for the selected segment and the toast action inside their 12–14px parents; 8px for checkboxes and the toast close; 6px for focus outlines; 4px for 12px legend swatches; 20px only for the login box. Pills (999px) for the rate chip, chips and status pills, the active tab pill, the floating action, the switch, and meter and range tracks; circles for row badges, Más icons, the tab dot and the range thumb. The WhatsApp preview is a speech bubble: 16px corners with the bottom-left at 4px.

The torta is the one circular silhouette: a gold board ring with a darker edge and a dashed frosting scallop, a white disc, and a 34-unit slice ring with 2.2-unit gaps.

### Torta (signature)
A top-down cake that shows where every dollar of a price goes, whole or per portion.

### Motion
One curve, `--salida` (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), for everything. State changes run 120–220ms (press scale 120ms, colours 140–180ms, tab pill and header line 200ms, switch knob 220ms). The authored movements are the torta slices (520ms), the goal and product bars filling by `scaleX` (600ms) and the toast rising (260ms); the save spinner turns in 0.9s. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` every transition and animation collapses to 1ms and the spinner stops.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** set every Bs amount in turquoise ink (`turquesa-tinta`) and every price in gold ink (`oro-tinta`); keep dollars in plum ink.
- **Do** use `fucsia-texto` for fuchsia text, links and focus, and `fucsia` only for filled surfaces with white on them.
- **Do** put `tinta-fija` on Sun Yellow and on fixed-white surfaces, so they read the same at night.
- **Do** edge cards, lists and blocks with a 1px `linea` and keep `--sombra-alta` for fixed actions, toasts and tooltips.
- **Do** mark income and expense with `+` / `−` signs and the row badge (plus on Fuchsia Blush, minus on Pressed Pink).
- **Do** keep the torta's slice order and colours exactly `serie-1` to `serie-6`, expenses in `serie-gasto`, and pair every cake with values in text.
- **Do** check every new colour pair in both themes; the dark tokens are part of the system, not an afterthought.
- **Do** keep the QR on a white ground in every theme.
- **Do** undo a delete from the toast rather than asking first.
- **Do** summarise with plain label/amount rows divided by a Pink Hairline.
- **Do** keep touch targets at 44px or more on phones and amounts in tabular numerals.
- **Do** write money as `$1.234,56` / `Bs. 1.234,56` and pick singular or plural from the count.
- **Do** keep state feedback to colour, background and press-scale in 120–220ms on `--salida`, and respect reduced motion.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use green for income or profit; red only for a real loss or an error. WhatsApp Green belongs to the WhatsApp icon alone.
- **Don't** set Bs amounts in any colour but turquoise ink, and don't use the bright `turquesa`, `oro` or `serie-*` hues as text.
- **Don't** set fuchsia text with the `fucsia` fill token, or text on yellow with `tinta`; both break at night.
- **Don't** add a shadow to a resting card, list, block or bar; the line is the elevation.
- **Don't** let the product torta stand without its legend table: orange and gold slices are below 3:1 against the light surface.
- **Don't** cycle, reorder or extend the chart series, reuse a slice hue for expenses, or swap merma back to a grey.
- **Don't** build stat tiles or KPI cards for the month's numbers.
- **Don't** spend Sun Yellow on decoration; it marks only the rate, the current place and the undo.
- **Don't** put payment details, account numbers or a QR image in the code; they come only from the owner's private settings.
